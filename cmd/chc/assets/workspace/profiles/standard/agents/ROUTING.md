# Routing

Classify work before acting.

## Request Types

- Question: answer without changing files.
- Alignment: preserve decisions and open questions.
- Planning: produce or update plan/tracker artifacts.
- Implementation: change files only after the task scope is clear.
- Review: prioritize defects, regressions, risks, and missing validation.

## Rules

- Do not treat discussion as permission to implement.
- Prefer explicit tickets or plans for mutating work.
- Route blockers to ticket comments, trackers, or reports when those artifacts exist.
