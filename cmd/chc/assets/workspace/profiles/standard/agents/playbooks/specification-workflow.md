# Specification Workflow

Use this playbook when behavior needs to become a stable product or technical contract.

## Drafts

Drafts capture evolving behavior, tradeoffs, and open questions.

## Specs

Specs capture stable contracts.

## Rules

- Keep drafts and specs in English.
- Separate model details when the spec introduces durable entities.
- Cross-reference related drafts, specs, tickets, and trackers.
