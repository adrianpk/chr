## Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning and boundary | Current implementation | Governing contract and regression protection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frontier | The confirmed prefix of an invocation's admitted order and its verified canonical delivery. Advancing it retains the unit receipt/proof, releases that finalized unit and hydrates the next execution target. The original admission target remains frozen. A set boundary also waits for confirmed closure. | [Frontier](../../../internal/core/workflow/workflow_frontier.go), `AdvanceWorkflowFrontier`, `Invocation.ExecutionSelection` | Sections 5 and 6; `TestWorkflowFrontierOrder`, `TestWorkflowFrontierRecovery`, `TestWorkflowContinuationDelivery` |
| Adoption | An explicit, quiescent binding of one approved legacy plan and exact selected task identities to native records. It reconciles identity, current source, scope and ownership. Reading, initialization and ordinary defaults do not adopt historical work. | [Adoption](../../../internal/core/workflow/workflow_adoption.go), [record binding](../../../internal/core/workflow/workflow_adoption_records.go) | Section 4; `TestWorkflowPlanAdoption`, `TestWorkflowPlanAdoptionRejects`, `TestWorkflowPlanAdoptionOwned`, `TestWorkflowUpgradeAdoptionRecovery` |
| Loose selection | An explicit queue of eligible independent tickets. Slice-owned tickets stay excluded even when filters match. Fields combine with AND, value lists with OR, and tags require every requested tag. Rank and creation/key ordering never reorder a slice. | [Selection](../../../internal/core/workflow/workflow_selection.go), `workflowSelection.matches`, `workflowSelection.resolveOrder` | Sections 5 and 7; `TestServiceWorkNative`, `TestServiceWorkTicketPreview`, `TestWorkflowCapacityQueue` |
| Continuation | Bounded accepted transitions on one retained invocation. It reuses checkpoints and frozen selection, target, filters, admission policy and retry allowances. Zero requested steps selects the frozen bound; it does not grant unlimited execution. A barrier returns before a successor starts. | [Continuation](../../../internal/core/workflow/workflow_continuation.go), `ContinueWorkflow`, `workflowContinuationLimit` | Section 5 and A24; `TestWorkflowContinuationBounds`, `TestWorkflowContinuationRecovery`, `TestWorkContinuePreparation` |
| Amendment | An explicit revision-guarded change to approved membership/order/contracts at a quiescent checkpoint. It may bind new tickets but preserves integrated tickets, prior revision maps and closure receipts. Later-admission policy does not substitute for amendment of an existing slice. | [Amendment](../../../internal/core/workflow/workflow_amendment.go), [amendment guards](../../../internal/core/workflow/workflow_amendment_guard.go) | Sections 3, 4 and 5; `TestWorkflowAmendmentRejects`, `TestWorkflowAmendmentLiveOwner`, `TestWorkflowAmendmentRecovery`, `TestWorkflowPlanOrderActiveAmendment` |
| Closing | A separate decision after all actual slices integrate. It freezes actual members, plan, validation contract and exact target, then validates an isolated checkout. Failure retries closure without replaying integrated tasks. Waiver records a reason and still requires member proof. Only guarded host confirmation closes the set. | [Closing](../../../internal/core/workflow/workflow_closing.go), [closeout](../../../internal/core/workflow/closeout/README.md) | Section 6 and A19; `TestWorkflowClosingActualMembers`, `TestWorkflowClosingEmpty`, `TestWorkflowClosingReopen`, `TestNativeClosingEvidence`, `TestWorkflowClosingPostObservation` |

`native` names the ticket-owned workflow and its host-validated contracts. It is
not a synonym for the forge transport. Admission, preparation, canonical
observation, publication and confirmation retain their distinct names and
states. `workflowContinuationStep` now names the shared accepted-step/barrier
result previously named only for preparation retry. Stored keys and enum strings
do not change with that internal rename.
